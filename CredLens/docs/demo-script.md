# CredLens Demo Script

1. Open a thin-file applicant.
2. Show traditional limitation: insufficient history.
3. Show CredLens Risk + Evidence Confidence + Trajectory + Integrity.
4. Show deterministic APPROVE/REFER/DECLINE.
5. Open Why? and show reason factors.
6. Open the precomputed Risk Journey.
7. Inject a live behavior event.
8. Show the updated risk and decision.
9. Show integrity status for the suspicious persona.
10. Show the Decision Audit Trail.

Use fixed rehearsed applicant IDs after data exploration rather than random browsing.
